W: Hello, everyone. Welcome to Study Smart. Have you ever stared at a huge project and felt so overwhelmed that you didn't know where to start? Many students give up before they even begin because the task seems too big. Here's my advice. Break the project into small, specific goals. Instead of saying, 'I'll write my research paper this week,' say, 'Today, I'll find three sources,' and 'Tomorrow, I'll write the introduction.' Each small goal is easy to reach, and finishing it gives you a sense of achievement that keeps you going. Step by step, the big project gets done. So the next time a task feels impossible, remember that small goals can carry you all the way to the finish line.
